A Rare Find — Bettie's Father
Cincinnati, Ohio · 1913

Cincinnati, OH · 1913

This rare photograph, taken in Cincinnati, Ohio in 1913, shows Bettie's father John Heck and his brother-in-law (Bettie's uncle) Herman Talbert on the back. This is believed to be a picture of the first 1913 V-Twin Harley Davidson in Cincinnati.

Bettie's father was a milkman who delivered milk with a horse and wagon for Webber Dairy of Cincinnati — and was also a great motorcycle enthusiast.
