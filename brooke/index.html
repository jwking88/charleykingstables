A Family Heritage

Charley King spent his life around horses — training, racing, and caring for them from his earliest days in Hamilton, Ohio to his final years at the Sharon Farm Stables in Crawfordville, Georgia. It's a joy to see that love of horses carry forward to the next generation of the King family.
